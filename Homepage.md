## 🚀 What Is Night City Reborn?

Night City Reborn is divided into **three modular collections**, allowing players to customise their experience:

### **🔧 NCR Core**
The main functional collection — stability, QoL, bug fixes, gameplay improvements, and essential enhancements.

### **✨ NCR Extras**
Optional additions such as vehicles, weapons, gameplay expansions, and visual upgrades.

### **🧬 NCR Body**
Optional body overhaul with compatible outfits and appearance‑related mods.

Each module is fully documented in this repository.
